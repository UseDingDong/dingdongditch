# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in DingDongDitch welcoming and harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of race, religion, gender, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas and technical outcomes, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Gracefully accept maintainer decisions and disengage from unproductive
  exchanges.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, trolling, and other
conduct that makes participation unsafe are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, close interactions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when they reasonably determine that this Code has been violated.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Enforcement decisions
may be reconsidered when material context was missed.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
